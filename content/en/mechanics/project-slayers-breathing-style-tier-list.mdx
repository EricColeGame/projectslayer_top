Every style is scored against the same checklist. A breathing style that deletes bosses might feel clunky in a 1v1, and a duelist's dream kit can be miserable for clearing mobs. That's why the rankings weigh several categories instead of raw damage alone.

| Criteria | Weight | What Reviewers Look For |
|---|---|---|
| Burst damage | High | How fast a full combo drains a health bar |

The practical takeaway: pick a style whose weaknesses you can cover with your clan and accessories, not one that only looks impressive in a highlight clip.

## S-Tier and A-Tier, Explained

**Sun Breathing** sits at the top because it pairs heavy burst with tools that punish anyone who stands still. It's the classic endgame flex, and the unlock path is deliberately long.

**Moon Breathing** trades a little raw speed for range and pressure. Its animations cover wide arcs, which makes it forgiving in group content and brutal in choke points.

**Shadow Breathing** is the style most players underestimate until they fight someone who knows it. The kit leans on repositioning and burst windows, so it rewards aggressive, mobile play.

**The A-tier group** is where most players should actually live. Thunder offers speed and quick combo access. Flame brings straightforward, high-impact pressure. Water is the safest all-purpose choice. Wind rewards timing with strong follow-ups, and Stone is the tanky, steady option for players who prefer trading hits over dodging them.

## How to Unlock, Master, and Swap Breathing Styles

Breathing styles aren't handed out randomly. You progress through a trainer, complete trials, and then grind mastery to unlock each move in the kit. The exact requirements shift with updates, so the steps below are the general pattern rather than a fixed checklist.

| Step | What To Do | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visit the Breathing Trainer | Choose from the starter-style pool |
| 2 | Clear the trial quest | Some styles sit behind prestige gates |
| 3 | Grind mastery points | Land moves on NPCs, bosses, and mobs |
| 4 | Unlock every move | Moves unlock at mastery milestones |
| 5 | Swap styles | Done at the trainer, usually for in-game currency |

A few practical habits make the grind shorter:

- Use your full rotation on cooldown instead of spamming one move.
- Farm training dummies or low-risk NPCs when you're testing a new combo.
- Bank currency before swapping — respeccing resets your mastery progress.
- Check the [Project Slayers experience page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/discover/?Keyword=Project%20Slayers) for update notes before committing to a new style.

Swapping is the most common mistake. Players chase whichever style tops a tier list that week, reset their mastery, and end up weaker than they started. Pick one style, push it to full mastery, and only swap when you've clearly outgrown it.

## FAQ

**What is the best breathing style in Project Slayers?**

By community consensus, Sun, Moon, and Shadow top most rankings because they combine burst damage, mobility, and combo potential. That said, Water and Flame remain the most recommended picks for newer players because they're far easier to learn and still perform well across nearly all content.

**Is the grind for an S-tier style worth it?**

If you're already comfortable with the game's combat rhythm, yes. Endgame styles typically require high prestige and long trial chains, so they're a poor first goal for a fresh account. Build your fundamentals on an accessible style first.

**How often does this tier list change?**

Every major balance patch can shuffle the middle of the list. The top tier usually stays recognizable, but B and C tier styles regularly move up or down after damage, cooldown, or animation adjustments. Recheck rankings after each significant update.

**Can I use more than one breathing style at a time?**

No. You equip a single breathing style, and switching resets your mastery progress with the old one. That's why experienced players commit to one kit per build rather than constantly chasing the flavor of the month.
