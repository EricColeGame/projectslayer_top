## Project Slayers Clans Explained: Families, Rarity, and Buffs

Project Slayers is a Roblox action RPG inspired by Demon Slayer, and it is hosted on the [official Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com/) where the game receives its updates. In this game, your family acts like a permanent passive perk. It does not replace your breathing style — it enhances it, sometimes dramatically.

Community reports describe clan assignment as randomized when you create a character, with rerolls available through a dedicated reroll item. Because the roll is luck-based, most players end up building around whatever they get rather than chasing a perfect start.

**What clan bonuses typically affect**

| Bonus category | What it usually does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Damage output | Boosts sword or breathing damage | Faster boss clears and PvP bursts |
| Survivability | Raises effective HP or reduces damage taken | Lets you survive raid mechanics |
| Mobility | Sprint speed, dash uptime, cooldown reduction | Wins chases and dodges |
| Breathing synergy | Buffs one specific breathing style | Enables niche but powerful builds |
| Utility | EXP gain, quest rewards, drop odds | Speeds up overall progression |

**Community-reported rarity bands**

| Rarity band | How often players report seeing it | General power level |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Most common result | Small baseline buffs |
| Uncommon | Regular | Noticeable but modest |
| Rare | Occasional | Strong and style-specific |
| Epic | Infrequent | Build-defining |
| Legendary | Very infrequent | Best-in-class passives |
| Mythical / Secret | Extremely rare | Elite, often meta-defining |

Exact drop rates and even the rarity labels can shift between updates, so treat the table above as directional rather than gospel. The safest rule is simple: the rarer the roll, the more likely it is to carry a meaningful passive.

## Project Slayers Clan Tier List: S-Tier to D-Tier

This ranking blends four factors: raw power of the passive, how flexible it is across builds, how easy it is to use, and how much value it delivers relative to how hard it is to roll.

**Overall ranking**

| Tier | Families commonly placed here | What players say |
|---|---|---|
| S | Tsugikuni, Kamado | Elite passives that fit almost any build |
| A | Rengoku, Tomioka, Shinazugawa, Himejima, Uzui | Strong, reliable, minor drawbacks |
| B | Kocho, Iguro, Kanroji, Tokito, Hashibira | Good in the right hands, niche outside of it |
| C | Agatsuma, Urokodaki, Kuwajima | Useful early, falls off later |
| D | Unlisted and no-clan starts | Minimal passive value |

### S-Tier: The Rolls Everyone Wants

- **Tsugikuni** — Associated with the strongest swordsman lineage in the source material, and player experience generally matches that reputation. Community reports consistently describe it as one of the hardest-hitting bloodlines available, which is why it anchors almost every serious Project Slayers clan tier list.
- **Kamado** — Often called the best all-rounder. It tends to reward aggressive play while still leaving room to survive long fights, making it a comfortable pick for both boss runs and PvP.

### A-Tier: Strong, Dependable, Slightly Narrower

| Family | Community-reported strength | Ideal for |
|---|---|---|
| Rengoku | Heavy damage leaning toward flame builds | Boss damage races |
| Tomioka | Balanced offense and defense | New and returning players |
| Shinazugawa | Rewards staying aggressive at low HP | Experienced PvP players |
| Himejima | Tanky, forgiving of mistakes | Solo grinding |
| Uzui | Mobility and speed pressure | Chases and escapes |

These families rarely feel like a wasted roll. If you land any of them, you can comfortably build around the passive instead of rerolling immediately.

### B-Tier: Build-Dependent but Rewarding

Kocho, Iguro, Kanroji, Tokito, and Hashibira show up frequently in community discussion as "good, if you commit." They tend to shine when your breathing style matches the family theme, and they feel noticeably weaker when it does not. If you enjoy a specific breathing style, a matching B-tier family will often outperform a mismatched A-tier one.

### C and D-Tier: Fine to Start, Worth Rerolling Later

Agatsuma, Urokodaki, and Kuwajima are perfectly playable while you are leveling and learning boss patterns. Their passives simply do not scale as hard into endgame content. Unlisted and clanless starts belong at the bottom because they give you almost nothing to build around.

A useful habit: before you reroll, ask what your build is missing. If you die constantly, a defensive family will improve your clear speed more than a damage family will. If you never die but bosses take forever, the opposite is true.

## How to Reroll Your Clan (and Mistakes to Avoid)

**Reroll checklist**

| Step | Action | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm your current family | Know what you are giving up |
| 2 | Decide your target tier | S or A only, or any matching family |
| 3 | Stock reroll items | Events and codes are the cheapest source |
| 4 | Reroll in one session | Easier to track results and stop points |
| 5 | Set a hard stop rule | Example: stop at first A-tier |
| 6 | Rebuild around the result | Adjust accessories and style to fit |

**Mistakes that waste rerolls**

- Rerolling without a target, then regretting a decent A-tier roll you threw away.
- Chasing S-tier indefinitely and burning through everything you saved.
- Ignoring style synergy — a matching B-tier family often beats a mismatched S-tier.
- Rebuilding your whole character before testing the new passive in a real fight.
- Trusting a single tier list video from an older update without checking the date.

Labeled community reports are useful, but they reflect player opinion, not developer numbers. Always sanity-check advice against your own damage and survival in an actual boss fight.

## FAQ: Project Slayers Clan Tier List Questions

**What is the best clan in Project Slayers?**

Most community rankings put Tsugikuni and Kamado at the top of a Project Slayers clan tier list. Tsugikuni leans toward raw damage, while Kamado is praised as the more flexible all-rounder. Either one fits a wide range of breathing styles.

**Does your clan actually matter in Project Slayers?**

Yes. Family passives stack with your breathing style, race, and accessories, so a strong roll can noticeably change your damage, durability, or mobility. That said, skill and build choices still matter more than a single passive.

**Should I reroll a B-tier clan?**

Only if you already have a clear target and spare reroll items. A B-tier family that matches your breathing style often performs better in practice than an A-tier family that does not.

**How often do clan tier lists change?**

They shift whenever the developers adjust passives or add new families. Treat any Project Slayers clan tier list — including this one — as a snapshot, and re-check it after major updates before spending reroll items.
