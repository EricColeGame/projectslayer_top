## What Is Gourd Training in Project Slayers?

In the source material that inspired the game, trainees inflate a gourd with one continuous breath until it cracks — the whole point being to expand lung capacity so Total Concentration Breathing can be held longer in a real fight. Project Slayers adapts that idea into a training activity that sits inside its wider progression loop.

Practically speaking, the gourd is a tool, not a weapon. You do not equip it and fight bosses with it. Instead, you complete training with it to improve the parts of your character that breathing-based combat depends on: stamina economy, sustained output, and the milestones that help you push into later content with confidence.

Two caveats worth remembering:

- Exact rewards, cooldowns, and requirements change with updates.
- Player experience shared on the [Project Slayers community wiki](https://project-slayers.fandom.com/wiki/Gourd_Training) and in Discord servers is usually the fastest way to confirm current numbers.

## What Gourd Training Rewards You With

The headline value of gourd training is that it strengthens the foundation your breathing style sits on. Rather than handing you a new ability, it makes the abilities you already have easier to sustain — which matters most in extended boss attempts where running dry gets you killed.

| Reward Type | What It Affects | Who Cares Most |
|---|---|---|
| Breathing performance | How long you can keep pressure on in a long fight | Breathing style mains |
| Progression gates | Some content assumes training milestones are done | Completionists and unlock hunters |
| Character XP and resources | Overall leveling speed and material stock | Everyone, especially mid-game players |
| Habit and timing | Cleaner inputs and fewer wasted swings | New players learning combat flow |

### Does it replace your breathing style or sword?

No. Treat gourd training as a multiplier on habits you already have. A player with sloppy timing and great breathing stats still loses to a player with clean timing and average stats. The training raises your ceiling — it does not play the game for you.

## Project Slayers Gourd Guide FAQ

**What is gourd training actually used for in Project Slayers?**

It is a breathing-focused training activity that reinforces the stats and habits behind sustained combat. Think of it as foundation work rather than a new ability: it makes your existing style last longer and hit more consistently in extended fights.

**Do I have to finish gourd training to unlock breathing styles?**

Not necessarily — breathing unlocks usually depend on a mix of quest progress, level, and specific tasks rather than one single activity. That said, some progression steps do reference training milestones, so check the current requirements in-game before assuming you can skip it entirely.

**How long does gourd training take to complete?**

There is no honest single answer, because it is designed to be spread across multiple sessions rather than finished in one sitting. Community reports describe it as a background task you chip away at while doing quests, not a wall you stand in front of for hours.

**Is gourd training worth it in 2026?**

If you main a breathing-focused build, yes — the compounding gains are the kind you feel in long boss attempts. If you play casually, do it in small bursts and treat it as free progress rather than a chore. Either way, this Project Slayers gourd guide should give you a clear enough map to decide how much of your playtime it deserves.
